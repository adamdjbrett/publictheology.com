The Public Theology newsletter gathers the week's new essays, responses and resources into a single email. It arrives once a week, never more, and it is free.

## How to sign up

1. Enter your email address in the form below and press **Subscribe**.
2. Check your inbox for a confirmation email from Buttondown, our newsletter service, and click the link inside it. Your subscription isn't active until you confirm. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, look in your spam or promotions folder.
3. That's it. The next weekly issue will come to you.

## Your email address

We use your address only to send the newsletter. We don't sell it or share it. Every issue has an unsubscribe link at the bottom, and one click takes you off the list.

Prefer a feed reader? Every post is also available by [RSS](/feed.xml). Questions about the newsletter can go to <editor@publictheology.com>.
