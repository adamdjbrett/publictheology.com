I am a doctoral student at United Lutheran Seminary, on a Public Theology and Ethics track focused on physical AI, with a research focus on artificial intelligence and robotics policy. My work asks how institutions can adopt emerging technology without displacing the human judgment, relational trust, and accountability that give their work meaning.
