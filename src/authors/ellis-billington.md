I'm a first year PhD student at United Lutheran Seminary. I am also a graduate of both the MDiv and MTS programs at Garrett-Evangelical Theological Seminary. 

My main research interests are in uncovering the theologies and rich spiritual lives of those who have long been overlooked in the field of queer theology. Most of my work focuses on the BDSM/kink community, the polyamorous community and sex workers. I strive for a queer theology that is sex-positive in the fullest sense of the word. I strive to lift up these marginalized voices in ways that authentically represent individuals and communities while honoring individuals’ right to anonymity and privacy, and I uphold the ethics of my institution, United Lutheran Seminary.
