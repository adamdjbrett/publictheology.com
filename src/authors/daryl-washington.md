Pastor Daryl M. Washington was born and raised in Brooklyn, New York where he attended local area public schools. He went on to further his education at Allegheny College in Meadville PA and graduated with a Bachelor of Science degree in Clinical Psychology.



Pastor Washington received his formal training at The Bethlehem Baptist Church (Spring House PA) under the leadership of Pastor Charles Warren Quann and went on to obtain a Master of Divinity from Lutheran Theological Seminary’s Urban Theological Institute.  During his season at Bethlehem Baptist Church, Pastor Washington was appointed to various positions of leadership within the church including Worship Leader and Outreach Minister. His other assignments included Deacon Training and mentoring newly licensed ministers.



After years of combined Interim Pastorships, Associate Pastorates and Youth Pastor experiences at various churches, God relocated Pastor Washington to the Washington DC area where he was called as Senior Pastor of the St Mary’s Baptist Church in NW Washington DC. Pastor Washington served the SMBC Family for over eight years. During his tenure he taught Discipleship versus Membership and the difference between  Religion and a Personal Relationship with Jesus Christ.



Pastor Washington is known for his practical teaching style and taking his congregation “deeper” in the Word. He has devoted himself to teaching and preaching the Good News of Jesus Christ whenever and wherever the opportunity arises.



Outside of his church duties, Pastor Washington has held positions on several Boards including  DCBC and Child Home & Community and Philadelphia Baptist Association and Lutheran Theological Seminary.  He is an active participant in the NW Washington DC community; a Member of Wednesday Clergy; The Missionary Baptist Ministers’ Conference; American Baptist Home Mission Society, Progressive National Baptist and a member of Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity Incorporated. 



He and his wife JoAnn have been married for over 30 years and are the proud parents of two adult children Ashley and Jennifer.



Pastor Washington’s favorite scripture is II Timothy 4:2 (NIV) Which reads “Preach the word; be prepared in season and out of season; correct, rebuke and encourage- with great patience and careful instruction”.
