Dr. James Jones is an ordained minister, scholar, and organizational leader with over three decades of pastoral and community leadership experience. He holds a Doctor of Ministry in Black Church Leadership and is currently pursuing a PhD in Public Theology and Witness at United Lutheran Seminary. His scholarship explores W.E.B. Du Bois, Howard Washington Thurman, Black democratic thought, public theology, and leadership formation within African American religious traditions. Dr. Jones serves as Executive Director of the Northeast Center for Human Development and is the founder of Speak Life Consulting. His work bridges theology, history, leadership, and public engagement.
