Hau Sian Suan is a PhD student in Public Theology and Witness at United Lutheran Seminary. Originally from Chin State'/' Myanmar'/' he previously served as a lecturer and Dean of Students at Tedim Theological College. His research explores decolonial public theology, Christian witness, and solidarity with marginalized communities, especially in Myanmar. He seeks to connect theological reflection with public responsibility.
