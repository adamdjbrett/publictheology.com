I am a Lutheran pastor in Michigan’s Upper Peninsula, serving four ELCA congregations in Ishpeming as pastor of Bridge Building Ministry. My work is transition: leaving and arriving, ministries beginning and ending, congregations merging and closing.

I came to it in Texas in 2020, first as an intern at Lord of Life Lutheran Church in Austin, where Holy Fork in the Road Ministries was born out of that congregation’s completed ministry, and then on the staff of the Bishop of the Southwestern Texas Synod, accompanying roughly twenty-six congregations through transition. Before ordination I served twenty-one years in the Navy as a Russian cryptologic linguist.

My research asks why change in a congregation registers as rupture when transition is the ordinary condition of every community. I am building a project that sets community-generated records alongside congregational registers and annual reports, to make a congregation’s own motion visible to itself. It sits where public theology, public humanities, and digital humanities meet.

I am a trained end-of-life doula through INELDA and a crisis counselor with the Trevor Project. I believe congregations are the experts in their own ministry contexts.
