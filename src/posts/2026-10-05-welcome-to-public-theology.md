Public Theology opens today. It is a new publication of public theology, public witness and shared resources: writing on how faith shows up in public life, and a growing collection of resources for anyone doing that work.

There is not much here yet. That is on purpose. Posts will arrive as they are written rather than on a schedule, and the resources will be added as they prove useful.

If you want to follow along, the [RSS feed](/feed.xml) is the way to subscribe. You can also find the site on [X](https://x.com/_publictheology), [Facebook](https://facebook.com/publictheologycom) and [Instagram](https://instagram.com/publictheologycom). Welcome.
