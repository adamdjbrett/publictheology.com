Dr James McGrath is the co-author of *Real Intelligence: Teaching In The Era of Generative A.I.* along with Dr. Ankur Gupta. In the book, they describe how Large Language Models (LLMs) work, discuss challenges with our current educational system, and gives concrete examples as to how LLMs like ChatGPT or Claude might be used in teaching. A free download of the book may be found [here.] ([https://pressbooks.palni.org/realintelligence/](https://pressbooks.palni.org/realintelligence/))
